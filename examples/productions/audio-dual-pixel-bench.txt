Audio + Dual Pixel Bench — 60 seconds

Required: P4 0.6.11 (fixes rejection of Emergency Node pixel timeline cues).
The local P4 source has this fix. No other firmware change is required.

1. Copy bench_sync.wav onto the AUDIO NODE SD card:
   /showduino/audio/bench_sync.wav
2. Keep the working Audio Node output setting (LINE for your headphone jack).
3. Import audio_dual_pixel_bench.shdo through your normal Studio production
   import/deploy workflow, select the production on Director, and press PLAY.
   A .shdo is an authoring file; do not rename it timeline.json.
4. Both programmable lines must already be configured for at least 10 pixels.
   This show uses their first 10 pixels and does not change saved counts.
   Audio GPIO5 status indicator remains managed by firmware.

Expected sequence:
2s: repeating four-note audio melody begins, volume 55%.
3s: Audio strip red; Emergency strip blue.
10s: Audio green; Emergency red.
17s: Audio blue; Emergency green.
24s: both rainbow.
33s: Audio cyan chase; Emergency amber chase in reverse.
42s: Audio purple breathe; Emergency green/cyan breathe.
51s: both white at moderate show brightness.
56s: audio stopped; both lines dark. Production completes at 60s.
There are short black gaps between phases. The Emergency line starts 200ms
after the Audio line to spread command traffic.

Emergency check: on a second run, press the physical Emergency Node button
during an effect. The timeline should stop and both configured pixel lines
should turn emergency white. Audio follows your existing emergency asset
configuration, not this bench melody. Release and clear using the normal
manual procedure; the production must not resume automatically.

Checked against the current SHDO v2 routes, effect names and 64-byte cue limit.
Not yet played on hardware. This targets the current Audio peer and all online
Emergency peers exposed by the estop-node-pixels route.
